Privacy Policy

How Bot Hanter processes information when monitoring permitted Telegram sources and searching public Threads content through authorized platform access.

This policy describes the Bot Hanter service, operated by its independent developer (referred to as “we”). It applies to data handled by the Bot Hanter monitoring system, not just to this website.

1. What is Bot Hanter?

Bot Hanter is a tool intended to help its authorized users discover publicly shared professional opportunities and requests for digital marketing and related services. Depending on platform permissions and availability, it monitors Telegram channels or groups that an authorized account can access, and searches public Threads posts using the official Threads API. Relevant results may be forwarded as notifications to subscribed Telegram users.

Bot Hanter is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Meta, Threads, Telegram, or GitHub.

2. What data we process

  • Public content: text of matched public posts or messages, public usernames or display names, post or message identifiers, timestamps, source or channel information, and public permalinks.
  • Bot subscribers: Telegram user or chat identifiers, selected notification categories, subscription status, and messages or commands sent to Bot Hanter.
  • Search and operational data: search phrases, classification labels, duplicate-detection values, delivery status, errors, and diagnostic logs necessary to operate the service.
  • Connected accounts: authorized platform account identifiers and API credentials needed for integrations. Tokens are kept in restricted server configuration and are not published on this website.

We do not claim access to private Threads posts, private messages, or accounts outside permissions granted by the relevant platforms. This website itself has no sign-up form, analytics scripts, or advertising cookies.

3. Why and how we process data

We process information to find relevant professional requests, classify the content by service category, remove duplicate results, send notifications to authorized bot users, keep subscriptions working, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service against misuse. Only source content that can lawfully be accessed under the relevant platform rules is eligible for processing.

Publicly posted information may still be personal data. We consider applicable privacy rights and platform restrictions when deciding whether and how to process it. The appropriate legal basis depends on the type of data and applicable law.

4. Who receives data

Matched opportunity information may be delivered to the Telegram accounts that have subscribed to applicable Bot Hanter notifications. Data is also handled by service providers needed for the functionality, including Telegram for message delivery, Meta/Threads for authorized API access, and infrastructure providers for secure server hosting. GitHub Pages hosts this public policy page and may process ordinary website access information under its own policies.

We do not sell personal data or use the collected information to build advertising audiences. We do not publish private API tokens or subscriber databases.

5. How long information is kept

Our intended production configuration keeps matched opportunity records for up to 90 days, technical diagnostic logs for up to 30 days, and subscription data until unsubscribe, account removal, or a verified deletion request. API credentials are removed when an integration is disconnected or no longer needed. Limited backups may persist for up to 30 additional days.

These periods are operational commitments for the planned production deployment and must be implemented and verified before Bot Hanter goes live.

6. Your choices, access and deletion

Telegram subscribers can stop receiving notifications by unsubscribing through the bot or requesting removal. A person whose publicly available content has been included in a notification may also contact us to request review, correction where possible, restriction, or deletion of stored copies, subject to applicable law and platform requirements.

To request data deletion: email the contact address below with the subject “Bot Hanter — Data Deletion Request.” Include a link to the post or the relevant Telegram identifier, if available, so we can find the record. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary sensitive information. We may ask for reasonable verification to protect other people’s data. We aim to respond to verified requests within 30 days or within any shorter period required by law.

7. Security and international processing

We intend to use HTTPS/TLS where applicable, access-controlled hosting, restricted access to credentials and stored records, and minimal administrative permissions. Data may be processed by service providers in countries other than where it was originally posted. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product, platform permissions, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. The current version will remain publicly accessible.
